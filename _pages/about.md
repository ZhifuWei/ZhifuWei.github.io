Hello! I am Zhifu Wei (<span class="zh-inline">魏智富</span> in Chinese), an incoming Ph.D. student (2027 cohort) at the [School of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science](https://saids.ustc.edu.cn/), USTC, advised by [Prof. An Zhang](https://anzhang314.github.io/). I am completing my undergraduate degree at [Northeastern University](https://www.neu.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Guibing Guo](https://guibingguo.github.io/).

My research interests focus on **large language models (LLMs)**, **AI agents**, and **recommender systems**. I am particularly interested in using LLMs and AI agents to enhance recommender systems, from recommendation modeling to automated research and development.

Please feel free to contact me by email if you have any questions or are seeking collaborations.
